Overview

PDFBench is a local-first macOS application developed by Go Studio Dev ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the PDFBench app, this website, and support communications handle data.

How PDFBench Works

PDFBench processes your PDF files locally on your Mac. We do not upload your documents, OCR results, or operation outputs to our servers.

Data We Do Not Collect In The App

PDFBench does not use in-app analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers. In particular:

  • We do not collect app analytics or behavioral profiles.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not transmit your PDF files or OCR content to external servers.

App Data Stored Locally On Your Device

PDFBench stores limited local data on your Mac using standard macOS mechanisms such as UserDefaults:

  • Preferences: theme, language, output folder, compression level, and OCR language settings.
  • Trial information: the date you first launched the app, used to calculate the trial period.
  • Operation history: a local history of operations you have run.

This information stays on your device unless you choose to include it in a support email or backup. We do not receive it automatically.

Website Data

These website pages are static informational pages. We do not intentionally use analytics, advertising pixels, or marketing cookies on this website.

Like most websites, basic technical data such as IP address, request path, user agent, timestamps, and security logs may be processed by hosting, CDN, DNS, or email providers as necessary to deliver and secure the website. The legal basis for that processing is our legitimate interest in operating and protecting the site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we process the information you send us, such as your email address, name, message content, and any attachments, in order to answer your request and provide support. The legal basis is your request for support and our legitimate interest in responding to product, legal, and customer-service inquiries.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple acts as the payment processor and merchant-of-record for the transaction flow presented in the App Store.

OCR (Optical Character Recognition)

Text recognition is powered by Apple's on-device Vision framework. All OCR processing happens locally on your Mac — no text or images are sent to any server.

Recipients And Service Providers

We do not sell personal data. Depending on how you interact with us, limited data may be handled by:

  • Apple: for App Store distribution, purchases, and app-page disclosures.
  • Infrastructure providers: for website hosting, DNS, CDN delivery, and security logging.
  • Email providers: if you contact us by email.

International Transfers

The app itself does not transfer your documents to us. However, support emails and website infrastructure may involve providers operating internationally. When this happens, the relevant provider's safeguards and contractual arrangements apply to that processing.

Retention

  • Local app data: retained on your device until you clear it or remove the app.
  • Support emails: retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, keep an audit trail, and manage follow-up support or legal obligations.
  • Website technical logs: retained according to the practices of the relevant infrastructure provider and only for operational or security purposes.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability for personal data that we process. For app data that stays only on your Mac, you can generally control or delete it directly on your device. For support emails or other communications, contact us at fmollea@gmail.com.

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

Children's Privacy

PDFBench is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the app.

Changes to This Policy
